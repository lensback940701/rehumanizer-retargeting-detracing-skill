# 06 Field adapters

The three moves are the same in every field; what counts as an adversary,
living material, a trace and a legitimate caution differs. Read the section
matching `PAPER_TYPE` (and the neighbouring one for mixed designs).

## Qualitative interview, ethnographic or field-based studies

- Adversary: a dominant explanation in the literature (e.g., structural,
  rational-choice, diffusion or policy-driven accounts) or a common assumption
  about the actors studied.
- Living material: verbatim quotes at turning points, dilemmas, self-naming,
  prices and quantities mentioned by actors, observed scenes, contradictions
  between talk and practice, counter-voices.
- Legitimate cautions: reported vs observed; recollection vs contemporaneous
  record; interview year vs event year; one actor's account vs corroborated
  account. Express through verbs ("recalled", "claimed", "the contract stated").
- Typical traces: source-first narration, code personae, per-paragraph
  self-report disclaimers, transcript-handling notes.
- Multi-person interviews: attribute speech by the transcript locator and the
  speaker label in that file, never by a similar file name; record the
  locator in the material bank.
- Gate: `--paper-type qualitative` (quotes and numbers both protected).

## Case study and policy/governance analysis

- Adversary: the official or textbook account of how a policy/program works;
  top-down vs bottom-up assumptions; implementation-gap clichés.
- Living material: documents with dates and amounts, decisive meetings,
  thresholds and eligibility rules, actors' tactical moves, unintended effects.
- Legitimate cautions: plan vs authorization vs delivery vs outcome; policy
  period vs project period.
- Typical traces: policy-list paragraphs, "relevant departments", safe generic
  substitution of program names.
- Gate: `--paper-type qualitative` or `mixed`.

## Quantitative empirical studies

- Adversary: a prevailing estimate, mechanism or modelling assumption; a
  "consensus" effect size; a neglected heterogeneity.
- Living material: the headline effect in meaningful units (money, time,
  people, area), the surprising sign or heterogeneity, a named illustrative
  unit, the counterfactual magnitude, the robustness result that rules out the
  main rival.
- Legitimate cautions: identification assumptions, external validity,
  measurement limits — stated where they bear on a specific estimate.
- Typical traces: "results show significant…" chains, coefficient narration
  without magnitude, robustness sections that read as self-defence, every
  sentence carrying "may".
- Rehumanizing here means stakes and units, not anecdotes; do not invent
  vignettes.
- Gate: `--paper-type quantitative` (numbers protected; quotes not required).

## Mixed methods

- Adversary may come from either strand; the payoff is usually where the
  strands disagree or where one explains the other.
- Living material: the number that sets the puzzle and the voice that explains
  it.
- Make sure integration happens in the findings, not only in the discussion.
- Gate: `--paper-type mixed`.

## Theoretical, conceptual or review articles

- Adversary: a named position, school or conceptual conflation.
- Living material: sharp exemplars, a well-chosen empirical illustration from
  the literature, a precise definition that does work, the author's own
  position stated plainly.
- Legitimate cautions: scope of the concept; what it does not cover.
- Typical traces: literature chains, "this review provides a framework",
  balanced summaries with no verdict.
- Gate: `--paper-type theoretical`; living-material checks fall back to numbers
  only, so the cold read must judge exemplars and stance.

## Historical, archival and humanities work

- Adversary: an established periodization, interpretation or canon.
- Living material: dated archival passages, named actors, the documentary
  surprise, material objects and places that carry the argument.
- Legitimate cautions: archival gaps, authorship and dating uncertainty,
  translation choices.
- Typical traces: archive-handling narration inside the argument, repeated
  "the sources suggest".
- Gate: `--paper-type qualitative`.

## Language notes

- Chinese social-science prose: sentences usually link claim, evidence and implication through clauses; keep relation words (因此、然而、由于、从而) and attribute once per passage. Watch 条件/安排/关系/环节/进入/范围/组织/实施
  stacks, 由此/因此/可见 wrap-ups, 而非/不能仅 negations, and role+code
  subjects such as “受访者（R2）”. Journals generally accept 本文认为 and
  direct quotation; use them.
- English prose: watch "rather than", "not merely", "is related to", "it
  should be noted", nominalization chains, and "respondents reported" as the
  default subject.
- Bilingual papers: route-correct the main-language text first, then rewrite
  the other-language abstract from it; do not translate the old abstract.
